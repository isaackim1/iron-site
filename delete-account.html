How to ask

Account deletion is handled by a person, by email. Nothing is deleted automatically.

Email iron.support.business@gmail.com

Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Iron, with the subject Iron account deletion request.

You can also start the email from the app: open Account, then Request account deletion. Iron opens your email app with the address and subject filled in. The request is only made once you send the email yourself.

You do not need to have the app installed to ask.

What happens next

  1. We check that the request comes from the email address linked to your Iron account. If it does not, we will ask you to confirm from that address. We will not ask for identity documents unless it is genuinely necessary.
  2. We offer you a copy of your data before it is deleted. You can also get a copy yourself in the app under Account, Get a copy of my data.
  3. We delete your account and the data listed below.
  4. We email you to confirm when the deletion is complete.

We act on requests without undue delay, normally within one month.

What is deleted

Deleting your account removes it from Iron's database, together with:

  • Your account and the email address you sign in with
  • Your display name and profile
  • Your group memberships
  • All your reflections, private and shared
  • Your Amen responses
  • Your reminder setting
  • Your consent records
  • Your community rules acceptance
  • Reports you sent, and reports about your account

What may be kept

  • Service-provider backups, if any, may keep temporary copies until their normal expiry. Iron will not restore an older backup unless completed deletion requests can be reapplied.
  • Administrative and deletion records that contain identifiers and times only, never reflection or prayer wording. At present there is no automatic deletion schedule for these records, so they are kept.
  • Our email conversation about your request, which stays in Iron's support mailbox. There is no fixed period for deleting it, so at present it is kept.

If you lead a group

A group's prayer point and schedule belong to the group, not to one person. If you are the only leader, we will ask whether you want to pass leadership to another member or close the group before your account is deleted. Closing a group removes its shared space, prayer point, schedule, Amens and invite codes; each member keeps their own reflections privately.

Deleting less than everything

Without deleting your account, you can do the following in the app at any time:

  • Remove your own Amen from a passage.
  • Edit a reflection, or change a shared reflection to private.
  • Leave a group. Your shared reflections leave the group with you and stay private.

There is no button to delete a single reflection. To ask for specific data to be deleted, email us as described above.

More detail is in the privacy policy.
