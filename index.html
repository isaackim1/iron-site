A quiet room for your group and the Word.

Iron helps an existing Christian small group read the Bible, pray and reflect together, one leader-set week at a time.

Coming to Google Play

How a week works

The leader sets the rhythm. Members take part without being scored, compared or pressured.

For the leader

  1. Create a group and share its invite code with people you know.
  2. Choose the reading days and a Bible chapter for each one.
  3. Add one prayer point for the group, then publish the week.
  4. Approve each request to join before they can see the group.

For members

  1. Enter the invite code and wait for the leader to approve you.
  2. Read the assigned passage on each reading day.
  3. Respond with Amen, or write a reflection.
  4. Keep the reflection private, or share it with your group.

An optional daily reminder, set on your phone at the time you choose, lets you know the reading is ready.

What Iron leaves out

Iron is for a group that already exists. It is not a social network, a public Bible platform or a way to measure anyone.

  • Public profiles
  • Discovery or search for groups
  • Likes and comments
  • Streaks and leaderboards
  • Faith or participation scores
  • Lists of who did not read
  • Advertising
  • Behavioural analytics

Privacy in brief

Reflections and prayer can reveal what you believe, so Iron treats them as sensitive. Iron is for adults aged 18 or older.

Read the privacy policy
Private reflections stay private
Only you can see a private reflection in the app. Leaders cannot see it.
Your email stays with you
Leaders and members never see the email address you sign in with.
Nothing is sold
Iron does not sell data, show ads or build advertising profiles.

Support

For help with Iron, privacy questions or data requests, email iron.support.business@gmail.com. Please write from the address you use to sign in, so we can find your account.

Report a concern

To report content or a person in your group, use Report in the app. Iron's operator reviews reports. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Delete your account

You can ask for your account and data to be deleted at any time. See how account deletion works.

Get Iron

Iron is for Android phones. It is not available on Google Play yet.

Bible text

Iron is in English. Its Bible text is the World English Bible, which is in the public domain.
