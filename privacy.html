Who is responsible

Isaac Kim, independent operator of Iron, is responsible for the personal information described here. If Iron later operates through a company, this policy will be updated before that company becomes responsible.

You can contact us at iron.support.business@gmail.com.

What Iron is

Iron is a leader-led space for existing Christian small groups. A leader chooses weekly Bible readings and one shared prayer point. Members read the passage, may respond with Amen, and may write reflections that they keep private or share with their group.

Because this can reveal religious beliefs, Iron treats the information as sensitive.

Iron is for adults aged 18 or older. Iron is not emergency care, counselling or pastoral care, it does not replace your local church, and it is not a place to report an immediate danger.

What we collect

  • Login and account data: email address, display name, language, and the times your account was created and your consent was recorded.
  • Community rules acceptance: your account identifier, the version of the rules and when you accepted them.
  • Reports you choose to send: your account, the group, the identifier of the reported item or person, the reason you selected, the time and the handling status. Prayer and reflection text is not copied into a report.
  • Group data: group membership, role, join requests, the reading schedule and leader actions.
  • Spiritual content you choose to provide: your reflections, your Amen responses to Bible passages, and the wording of the group prayer point.
  • Preferences: whether the daily reminder is on and the time you chose.
  • Minimal technical and security data needed to run and protect the service, such as app version, error category and the times of administrative events.

Iron does not ask for your legal name, phone number, home address, exact date of birth, gender, contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Iron does not include an advertising, analytics or crash-reporting service.

How we use it

We use this information only to run Iron for you and your group: to sign you in, to show your group its readings, prayer point, Amens and shared reflections, to keep your private reflections for you, to set your reminder, to answer your requests and to keep the service secure.

Before you create or join a group, Iron asks you to confirm you are 18 or older and to give explicit consent to Iron processing information that can reveal your religious beliefs. These are two separate choices, and neither is ticked for you. You can withdraw consent at any time (see Your rights).

Who can see what

  • Your login email is private. Group leaders and members do not see it.
  • A private reflection is visible only to you in the app. It is not end-to-end encrypted. The operator may technically access database records only when strictly necessary for security, a lawful request or a service incident; such access is limited and logged.
  • A shared reflection is visible only to the group you chose. You can edit it or make it private. A leader cannot change its wording but may hide it from the group; it then stays with you as a private reflection.
  • Amen applies only to Bible passages, never to someone's reflection. The display names of people who said Amen to a passage are visible inside the group. You can remove your own Amen.
  • Leaders can see the member list, roles, the schedule, the prayer point, shared reflections and who said Amen to each passage. They cannot see login emails, private reflections, drafts, reminder settings or a list of people who did not take part.
  • Reports can be read only by the operator. Leaders, other members and the person who sent the report cannot read report records in the app.

Joining and prayer points

An invite code creates a request to join. A leader must approve it before you can see any group content, including the group's name.

If you leave a group or are removed, your access ends immediately. Your shared reflections disappear from the group and stay with you as private reflections.

If a group is closed, its shared space, prayer point, schedule, Amens and invite codes are removed. Each author keeps their own reflections privately.

A prayer point can be marked "Group only" or "Shareable outside the group". You may share details about yourself. Do not identify another person without their permission; if you are unsure, keep the wording anonymous. Once something is shared outside Iron, Iron cannot control that copy.

Reminders and email

Iron offers an optional daily reminder at a time you choose. It is scheduled on your phone, not sent from a server. Iron asks for notification permission only when you turn the reminder on. The reminder text is generic and never shows prayer points, reflections, group names or email addresses.

On Android, a reminder may arrive later than the chosen time unless you allow Iron to use "Alarms & reminders" in your phone's settings.

Iron does not send notifications about other people's reflections or Amens.

Your email address is used to sign you in with a one-time code, and for security, privacy and essential service messages. It is not added to a marketing list. An account that already has a password can also use Sign in with password.

Bible text

This edition of Iron is English only and contains no Korean Scripture. Every Bible reading is the English World English Bible, including on a device that previously used Korean.

English Bible chapters are the World English Bible, a public-domain translation. Iron does not bundle the English text. When an English chapter is not already stored on your phone, the app requests it directly from the Free Use Bible API (bible.helloao.org), a third-party service, and keeps a copy on your phone once it has loaded. Stored chapters can be read without another request.

When a leader publishes a week, the app may load that week's chapters in advance so they are ready to read, including offline.

The provider receives the requested translation, book and chapter in the web address, your device's IP address and request information such as the time. A request does not show that you read the chapter and is not tied to your reminder time. Iron does not add your email, account or group identifiers, reflections, prayer points, Amens or sign-in token to these requests.

The provider's logging, retention periods and processing locations have not been verified. We do not promise that it keeps no logs or processes requests only in the EEA.

Content safeguards

Iron is used by closed groups. A leader approves who joins, can remove a member, and can hide a shared reflection from the group. Hiding a reflection records that it happened, without copying its text.

Community rules. Before you post names, group descriptions, prayer points, announcements or shared reflections, Iron asks you to agree to its community rules. This is separate from your privacy consent. You can read the rules at any time under Account.

Reporting. You can report a shared reflection, a prayer point or announcement, a group, or any member of your group, including the leader, using Report in the app. You choose a reason before sending. Isaac Kim, Iron's operator, reviews reports and may restrict content or account access where needed. Private reflections cannot be reported. There is a daily limit on new reports.

Iron has no personal block list, because there are no public profiles, direct messages or mentions. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Reports go to a list that only the operator can read. Nobody is alerted automatically when a report arrives, and Iron does not promise a response time.

No pressure, no profiling

Iron does not create streaks, leaderboards, faith scores, lists of people who did not take part, or reminders that name anyone. Iron does not sell personal, religious or behavioural data, build advertising profiles, train AI on your spiritual content or give churches behavioural analytics about individuals.

Security

Iron uses access controls, authentication and least-privilege administration. Access rules are enforced in the database for every request, not only in the app. No system is perfectly secure.

If a breach affects you, Iron will explain what happened and what we are doing about it, and will notify the authorities or you when the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your account data, reflections, Amens and preferences while your account exists. Group data, such as the schedule and prayer point, is kept while the group is active.

There is no button to delete a single reflection. You can edit a reflection, make it private, or remove your own Amen at any time.

You can ask us to delete your account and its data. Read how account deletion works.

Service-provider backups, if any, may keep temporary copies until their normal expiry. Iron will not restore an older backup unless completed deletion requests can be reapplied.

Iron keeps a small number of administrative records, such as when a member was approved or removed, and records of completed account deletions. They contain identifiers and times only, never reflection or prayer wording. At present there is no automatic deletion schedule for these records, so they are kept.

Your community rules acceptance is deleted with your account. A report is deleted when the account that sent it or the group is deleted, and when the reported account, reflection or schedule is deleted. Otherwise, reports are not deleted on a schedule, so at present they are kept.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, receive a copy of, or delete your data. Where applicable, you can also ask us to restrict or stop processing, or withdraw your consent. Withdrawing consent does not undo earlier lawful processing. Because group features depend on consent, withdrawing it may mean leaving your groups or deleting your account.

You can get a copy of your own reflections and Amens yourself in the app under Account, Get a copy of my data. It does not include other people's content, your community rules acceptance or reports you sent. You can ask for a copy of those by email.

To make any other request, email us from the address linked to your account. We verify requests using that address and answer without undue delay, normally within one month. We will not ask for identity documents unless it is genuinely necessary.

You may also be able to complain to a data protection supervisory authority where you live.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and update the notice in the app. If a change affects how Iron processes information that can reveal your religious beliefs, Iron will ask for your consent again in the app before you continue.
